AMDF: Holistic Probing of Vision Foundation Models — full results

ML in PL 2026 · Turhan Can Kargin · turhancan.kargin@doctoral.uj.edu.pl

Setup & protocol

  • Backbones (frozen): DINOv3-S/B/L, MAE-L, SigLIP2-L, V-JEPA2.1-L, PESpatial-L; all ViT blocks used.
  • Benchmarks: NYUv2 surface normals, depth (log-depth head), semantic segmentation; Taskonomy (aggregate L₁); NAVI camera pose.
  • Baselines: LastLayer; AvgLayer; WeightedLayers (one learned layer mix); SpatialWeights (learned mix per patch position, input-independent); ConcatLayers (concat → 1×1 projection + GELU).
  • AMDF: per patch, 16 experts with per-layer queries; keys Wk(hℓ+pℓ) in 64-d, softmax over layers (/√64); values Wvhℓ at full width, split in 16 blocks; no bottleneck, no per-layer refinement, no MLP in the probe.
  • Heads: dense tasks — linear 1×1 conv then bilinear upsampling; pose — pool both images, concatenate, MLP. Identical for all methods within a table.
  • Protocol: same recipe for every row of a table; mean of up to 3 seeds (8/42/123; a few baseline cells have 1–2); best-epoch validation metric.
  • Ablation (per-layer vs shared queries): paired gains, same seeds, mean over 7 backbones — normals +1.62 d₁ (7/7), Taskonomy −0.73 L₁×100 (7/7), seg +0.16 mIoU (6/7), depth +0.51 d₁ (5/7), pose −2.37° rot. (5/7; mostly SigLIP2-L, −13°); cost +23.5k params (≈2%).
